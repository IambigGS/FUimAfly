# Visual PRD: Wasp Attack Mini-Game
**Lead Art Director:** Luna

## 1. Overview
The Wasp Attack mini-game introduces a sudden, high-intensity visual shift. The aesthetic should contrast with the serene, traditional look of the standard chopstick fly-catching game. When the wasp is caught, the game transforms into a dynamic, pseudo-3D head-on perspective.

## 2. Transition Effect (The "Oh No" Moment)
- **Visual Freeze & Flash:** The moment the chopsticks grab the wasp, the game freezes for 0.5 seconds. A subtle chromatic aberration effect pulses, accompanied by a sudden red or danger-yellow vignette around the screen borders.
- **Camera Zoom & Rotate:** The camera aggressively zooms in on the caught wasp, causing a blurring radial effect. The screen then dramatically "spins" or flips 180 degrees, simulating the master's perspective shifting from looking down to looking straight ahead.
- **Environment Shift:** The background fades into a slightly more intense, dynamically lit setting (e.g., darker shadows, subtle speed lines moving toward the camera) while adhering to our clean neutral light gray (#d1d5db / #e5e7eb) studio background principles to keep the action readable.

## 3. Head-On Perspective (Pseudo-3D)
- **Viewport:** The camera is positioned at the master's eye level.
- **Foreground Elements:** The master's hands wielding the chopsticks are visible in the lower corners of the screen in a first-person view, ready to snap shut.
- **Depth of Field:** A shallow depth of field is used; the background is blurred to keep absolute focus on the wasps zooming in.

## 4. Wasp Animations
- **Flight Path:** Wasps spawn as small dots in the center-distance and scale up aggressively as they fly directly towards the screen, creating a z-axis pseudo-3D effect.
- **Animation cycle:** Wasps have a rapid, aggressive wing-flap cycle. Their flight paths shouldn't be perfectly straight; they should bob and weave slightly (sine wave motion) to make targeting challenging.
- **Strike Frame:** When a wasp gets within striking distance, it briefly rears back, showing its stinger, before lunging.
- **Defeat:** When caught by the chopsticks, the wasp emits a satisfying "splat" or crunch particle effect (small yellow/black confetti or stylized geometric shards) and instantly disappears.
