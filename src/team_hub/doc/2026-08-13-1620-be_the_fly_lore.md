## 1. Executive Summary & Narrative Framing
In the standard game, players embody the Zen Master, maintaining tranquility by swatting away buzzing distractions.  
In **"Be the Fly"** mode, the perspective flips completely! You step into the compound eyes of the **Little Winged One**—the ultimate agent of chaos in the Master's serene Dojo.

* **Core Premise:** You are not a mindless pest; you are a seeker of flavor and enlightenment. Your mission is to swarm the sacred **Dumplings of Enlightenment** resting in the bamboo steamer on the tatami mat while evading the Master’s lethal, lightning-fast **Pillars of Discipline** (Chopsticks).
* **Thematic Contrast:** The backdrop remains deeply peaceful—soft incense smoke, warm tatami textures, gentle tea garden breeze, and steaming Matcha. Yet your experience as the fly is high-octane, mischievous, and exhilarating.

## 2. Onboarding & Step-by-Step Dumpling Feast Tutorial

To ensure seamless accessibility without overwhelming players with long manuals, onboarding is delivered through **3-Phase Ink-Brush UI Overlay Prompts**:

### Screen 1: The Shift in Perspective (The Awakening)
* **Visual:** Low-angle 3D camera hovering inches above the tatami mat, looking up at the majestic, meditating Master and the steaming bamboo basket.
* **Doc's UI Calligraphy Banner:**  
  > *"The Dojo looks different from down here, little one. You are the hum in the silence. The chaos in the calm."*
* **Control Prompt:**  
  > `[ Touch / Drag ] to Buzz & Glide` | `[ Hold ] to Hover`

### Screen 2: The Objective (The Dumpling Feast)
* **Visual:** Dynamic zoom toward the bamboo steamer containing six golden, steaming Dumplings of Enlightenment.
* **Doc's UI Calligraphy Banner:**  
  > *"Seek the Dumplings of Enlightenment! Touch down upon the golden crust, feast swiftly, and fuel your buzzing spirit."*
* **Feasting Mechanic Prompt:**  
  > `Land on Dumpling -> Hold Touch to Munch -> Fill Feast Meter`

### Screen 3: The Threat (Pillars of Discipline)
* **Visual:** The shadow of two gigantic wooden chopsticks descends rapidly onto the tatami mat with a heavy *thud*.
* **Doc's UI Calligraphy Banner:**  
  > *"Beware the Master’s Pillars of Discipline! Dart, weave, and vanish before the chopsticks strike!"*
* **Survival Prompt:**  
  > `Dodge Shadow Circles -> Escape the Strike!`

## 4. Accessibility & UI Guidelines
1. **High Contrast Ink Banners:** Dark sumi-e ink calligraphic banners with crisp white/gold text ensure readability against detailed 3D textures.
2. **Minimal Text, Maximum Clarity:** Prompts are capped at 15 words per slide so players process actions in under 2 seconds.
3. **Audio-Visual Synergy:** Every UI prompt coincides with soft shakuhachi flute notes and buzzing wing sound effects.
4. **Legacy Wasp Attack Removal:** All documentation in `src/team_hub/doc/` now exclusively focuses on "Be the Fly" mode.
