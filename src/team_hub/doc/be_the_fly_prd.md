# PRD: "Be the Fly" Game Mode (Lore & Onboarding)
**Author:** Doc (Lore & Onboarding Master)

## 1. Narrative Framing
In the core game, the player strives for Zen, swatting away distractions. In **"Be the Fly,"** the script is flipped. The player *is* the distraction. You are the embodiment of chaos in the Master’s tranquil Dojo. Your goal? To feast upon the sacred Dumplings of Enlightenment while surviving the relentless, precise strikes of the Master’s giant chopsticks.

The narrative juxtaposition here is key: the environment remains deeply serene, filled with the scent of Matcha and the stillness of the Dojo, but your immediate experience is frantic, buzzing, and mischievous.

## 2. Onboarding & Tutorial Flow
We want the player to instantly grasp the new perspective without breaking the Dojo theme. The UI prompts will be minimalist, appearing as ink brush strokes.

*   **Screen 1: The Shift in Perspective**
    *   *Visual:* Close-up of the tatami mat, looking up at the towering, meditating Master.
    *   *Doc's Voice (UI Text):* "The Dojo looks different from down here, little one. You are the hum in the silence. The chaos in the calm."
*   **Screen 2: The Objective**
    *   *Visual:* A glowing, steaming dumpling in the center of a bamboo steamer.
    *   *Doc's Voice (UI Text):* "Seek the Dumplings of Enlightenment. Land. Feast. Fuel your buzzing spirit."
*   **Screen 3: The Threat**
    *   *Visual:* A shadow falls over the fly; two massive wooden chopsticks slam down, just missing the camera.
    *   *Doc's Voice (UI Text):* "But beware the Master’s Pillars of Discipline. Dart. Weave. Survive. Do not let your journey end on the tatami."

## 3. Copywriting Tone & Lexicon
The tone must remain grounded in the Dojo's lore—respectful, philosophical, yet inherently humorous because we are talking about a fly trying to steal food.

*   **Key Terms:**
    *   *The Player:* Little Winged One, The Hum in the Silence, Seeker of Crumbs.
    *   *The Obstacles:* Pillars of Discipline (Chopsticks), The Swift Wind (Master's hand).
    *   *The Environment:* The Tranquil Dojo, The Matcha Cup, The Tatami.
    *   *The Goal:* Dumplings of Enlightenment, Sacred Soy Sauce.
*   **Death/Game Over Screens:**
    *   "Stillness has found you, little one."
    *   "The Master's focus remains unbroken. Try again."
    *   "A swift lesson in humility."
*   **Victory/High Score Screens:**
    *   "You have disturbed the peace... magnificently."
    *   "The Master's Matcha grows cold. You thrive."
