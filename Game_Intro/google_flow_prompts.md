# Cinematic Game Intro - Google Flow Prompts
*Designed by Luna (Art Director)*

The following prompts have been specifically engineered for Google Flow (or similar generative AI video models) to create a seamless 4-part cinematic intro that transitions directly into the top-down 2D gameplay.

---

### Clip 1: The Serene Feast
**Prompt:** A cinematic, photorealistic shot inside a traditional Japanese Zen room with sliding shoji screens open to a garden with gently falling cherry blossoms. A serious, calm martial arts master sits cross-legged at a low wooden table. On the table sits a steaming bamboo steamer basket filled with delicious dumplings. He holds wooden chopsticks, peacefully preparing to eat.
**Camera Directions:** Slow, smooth tracking shot starting on the cherry blossoms outside, panning into the room, and slowly pushing in on the master. 
**Aesthetic Notes:** Tranquil, soft natural sunlight. Warm wood tones, soft pinks, and shallow depth of field with cinematic bokeh in the background. Around 6 seconds.

### Clip 2: The Intruder
**Prompt:** A macro close-up shot of the steaming dumplings inside the bamboo basket. The steam swirls gently in the warm light. Suddenly, a large, detailed fly darts aggressively into the frame, hovering menacingly over the food. 
**Camera Directions:** Static close-up on the food, with the fly moving dynamically across the frame. A slight, quick camera shake as the fly buzzes close to the lens.
**Aesthetic Notes:** High contrast on the fly's silhouette to make it stand out against the soft, warm tones of the food. Fast shutter speed to capture the frantic wing movement, disrupting the previous tranquility. Around 6 seconds.

### Clip 3: The Master's Focus
**Prompt:** An extreme close-up of the master's face. His calm expression shifts instantly to one of intense, razor-sharp focus. His eyes narrow as they track the fast-moving fly off-screen. With a swift, fluid motion, he raises his wooden chopsticks into a striking, defensive stance near his face.
**Camera Directions:** A slow, tense push-in directly on his eyes, accompanied by a very subtle Dutch angle shift to build dramatic tension.
**Aesthetic Notes:** Dramatic, high-contrast directional lighting highlighting the determination in his eyes and the wood grain of the chopsticks. The mood shifts from peaceful to high-stakes action. Around 6 seconds.

### Clip 4: The Arena (Transition to Gameplay)
**Prompt:** A high-angle, top-down view looking directly down at a plate on the wooden table. The master's hands, holding the chopsticks, enter the frame from the bottom edge, poised to strike. Shadows of flies begin to dart across the wooden table. The framing perfectly mimics a top-down 2D arcade game perspective.
**Camera Directions:** The camera starts over-the-shoulder and sweeps dramatically upward and forward in a fluid motion, locking into a rigid, perfect 90-degree bird's-eye view looking straight down at the plate.
**Aesthetic Notes:** As the camera locks into the top-down view, the lighting should flatten slightly to match the 2D game environment. The wooden table serves as the arena backdrop, and the chopsticks frame the bottom perfectly, ready to instantly transition into the gameplay state. Around 8 seconds.
