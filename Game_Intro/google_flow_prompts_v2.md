### Clip 1: The Serene Feast
**Prompt:** A 2D anime style animation of a traditional Japanese Zen room with sliding shoji screens open to a garden with gently falling cherry blossoms. A grumpy martial arts master sits cross-legged at a low wooden table. He is drawn with clean, bold inked outlines, flat vibrant colors, and cel shading. On the table sits a steaming bamboo steamer basket filled with dumplings. He holds wooden chopsticks, peacefully preparing to eat.
**Camera Directions:** Slow, smooth 2D tracking pan starting on the cherry blossoms outside, panning into the room, and slowly scaling in on the master. 
**Aesthetic Notes:** High-quality 2D animation, hand-drawn anime/manga comic illustration style, flat vibrant colors, bold outlines, serene mood with gently drifting 2D cherry blossom petals. Around 6 seconds.

### Clip 2: The Intruder
**Prompt:** A 2D anime style close-up shot of the steaming dumplings inside the bamboo basket, featuring bold outlines and cel shading. The steam swirls gently. Suddenly, a large, stylized fly darts aggressively into the frame, hovering menacingly over the food. 
**Camera Directions:** Static close-up on the food, with the 2D fly moving dynamically across the frame. A slight, quick 2D camera shake as the fly buzzes close to the lens.
**Aesthetic Notes:** High contrast and vibrant flat colors. Fast, snappy 2D animation timing to capture the frantic wing movement, disrupting the previous tranquility. Around 6 seconds.

### Clip 3: The Master's Focus
**Prompt:** An extreme 2D anime close-up of the master's face. His calm expression shifts instantly to an exaggerated, intense anime expression of anger—his eyes narrow, thick manga speedlines appear in the background, and he adopts a grumpy martial arts master look. With a swift, fluid motion, he raises his wooden chopsticks into a striking, defensive stance near his face.
**Camera Directions:** A slow, tense push-in directly on his eyes, accompanied by an intense anime dramatic zoom.
**Aesthetic Notes:** Dramatic cel shading, thick bold outlines, exaggerated facial expression, dynamic manga-style action lines in the background. The mood shifts from peaceful to high-stakes anime action. Around 6 seconds.

### Clip 4: The Arena (Transition to Gameplay)
**Prompt:** A high-angle, top-down 2D view looking directly down at a plate on the wooden table. The master's hands, drawn in 2D anime style with bold outlines, enter the frame from the bottom edge holding the chopsticks, poised to strike. Stylized 2D shadows of flies begin to dart across the wooden table. The framing perfectly mimics a top-down 2D arcade game perspective.
**Camera Directions:** The virtual 2D camera sweeps dramatically upward and forward in a fluid motion, locking into a rigid, perfect 90-degree bird's-eye view looking straight down at the plate.
**Aesthetic Notes:** Top-down 2D video game aesthetic. Flat, vibrant cel-shaded colors, clean inked outlines. The wooden table serves as the 2D arena backdrop, and the chopsticks frame the bottom perfectly, ready to instantly transition into the gameplay state. Around 8 seconds.
