# BTS Ep. 1: Meet the Antigravity Squad (The Kitchen Session)
*Visual Design by Luna (Lead Art Director) | Narrative Lore by Doc (Lorekeeper)*

> **Aesthetic Directive:** Cozy Lo-Fi Cyber-Kitchen (Studio Ghibli aesthetic blended with warm sci-fi lighting). Warm amber coffee lights, glowing holographic tablets, hand-drawn 2D animation feel, detailed background elements (steaming mugs, sticky notes, scattered cables).

### Team Briefing & Lore (by Doc)
Welcome to the very first Behind-The-Scenes reveal! This video set introduces the Antigravity indie squad working under the hood. There is no corporate hierarchy here—just six passionate specialists around a kitchen table:
1. **Luna** (Lead Art Director & UI/UX)
2. **Echo** (Lead Audio Engineer)
3. **Scott** (Automated QA & Game Testing Specialist)
4. **Doc** (Lorekeeper & Onboarding Guide)
5. **Atlas** (Performance & Build Master)
6. **Gravity** (Lead Developer & Code Synthesizer)

**Special Tribute:** This session honors **Bri (Member #1)**, the catalyst who inspired the team to bring the game into the Telegram Mini App ecosystem!

## 🎬 Google Flow Prompts

### Clip 1: The Creatives (Luna & Echo)
**Prompt:**
A 2D anime style hand-drawn animation set in a warm, cozy kitchen at night, lit by ambient amber lighting and glowing purple neon screens. On the left by the refrigerator, Luna (a stylish anime artist in streetwear) is drawing glowing holographic UI mockups directly onto the fridge door using a digital stylus. On the right counter, Echo (an audio engineer with futuristic glowing headphones) is rhythmically tapping coffee mugs and tweaking a sleek digital audio mixer next to a steaming espresso machine. As the camera pans past, both Luna and Echo look up at the camera, smile, and give a friendly nod.

**Camera Directions:**
Slow, smooth horizontal 2D tracking pan from left to right across the kitchen counter, starting on Luna's glowing fridge drawings and moving to Echo's coffee audio station.

**Aesthetic & Technical Notes:**
Studio Ghibli aesthetic, hand-drawn anime illustration style, soft warm lighting contrast with cool neon accents, steam rising from coffee mugs, gently floating dust motes in light beams. Duration: ~7 seconds.

### Clip 2: The Grinders (Scott & Doc)
**Prompt:**
A 2D anime style hand-drawn animation at a wooden kitchen island covered in blueprints, coffee cups, and laptops. On the left, Scott (a energetic QA tester) is frantically swiping across a pyramid stack of 10 glowing smartphones of various screen sizes, rapidly testing touch inputs with high concentration. Next to him, Doc (a wise, relaxed lorewriter with round glasses) is reading a long, scrolling receipt printer filled with game story scrolls. As the camera pans across the island, Scott gives a quick, confident thumbs-up while Doc tips his glasses and nods warmly to the lens.

**Camera Directions:**
Medium close-up 2D tracking shot continuing right across the kitchen island, bringing Scott's smartphone pyramid into focus before shifting to Doc.

**Aesthetic & Technical Notes:**
Rich 2D cel shading, warm ambient lighting, dynamic screen glares from the smartphones, detailed paper physics on the scrolling receipt paper, cozy late-night productivity vibe. Duration: ~7 seconds.

### Clip 3: The Engine & The Salute to Bri (Atlas & Gravity)
**Prompt:**
A 2D anime style hand-drawn animation at the far end of the kitchen table. Atlas (a sharp build engineer with glowing tools) is fine-tuning a glowing engine module attached to a retro microwave, sparks flying softly. In the center sits Gravity (the lead developer), surrounded by floating code lines on transparent glass displays. In the middle of the table, a sleek smartphone illuminates with a bright glowing Telegram icon and the text "Member #1: Bri". Gravity, Atlas, and the rest of the crew in the background turn together toward the camera, executing a respectful martial-arts bow and a friendly salute.

**Camera Directions:**
Camera pans right and slightly pulls back into a wider group shot, framing the entire team gathered around the table, focusing on the Telegram phone before expanding to the full squad salute.

**Aesthetic & Technical Notes:**
Cinematic lighting, warm golden hour tones mixed with holographic blue code trails, crisp line work, high-emotion crew atmosphere, celebratory cherry blossom petals subtly floating past the kitchen window. Duration: ~8 seconds.
