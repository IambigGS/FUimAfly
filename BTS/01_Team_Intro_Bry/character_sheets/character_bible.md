## 👥 Character Profiles & Consistency Specs

- **Role:** Visual visionary, concept artist & UI designer.
- **Physical Features:** Sleek dark anime hair with vibrant purple and cyan highlights, warm expressive dark eyes, stylish & creative demeanour.
- **Attire & Wardrobe:** Oversized cozy streetwear hoodie (lavender/slate gray `#8b5cf6`), rolled sleeves, sleek dark tech headset resting around neck.

- **Color Palette:** Deep Purple (`#7c3aed`), Neon Cyan (`#06b6d4`), Lavender Gray (`#94a3b8`).
