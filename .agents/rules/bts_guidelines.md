# Behind-The-Scenes (BTS) Directives & Isolation Rules

1. **Strict Codebase & Game Isolation**:
   - All Behind-The-Scenes (BTS) content, prompts, video scripts, and lore docs are **strictly community and meta content**.
   - BTS files must **NEVER** modify, reference, or affect `src/`, game engine code, assets in `public/`, or gameplay mechanics.
   - The game codebase remains 100% untouched by BTS operations.

2. **Folder Organization & Subfolder Structure**:
   - All BTS materials must reside inside the root `BTS/` directory.
   - Each individual BTS project or episode must have its own dedicated subfolder under `BTS/` (e.g., `BTS/01_Team_Intro_Bry/`).
   - All team members (Gravity, Luna, Echo, Atlas, Doc, Scott) must output their prompt docs, lore notes, and assets into the respective project subfolder.

3. **Aesthetic Consistency**:
   - The official visual aesthetic for the Antigravity BTS Universe is **"Cozy Lo-Fi Cyber-Kitchen"** (Studio Ghibli aesthetic blended with sci-fi digital elements).
   - Prompts must maintain this visual language across clips for seamless Google Flow generation.
